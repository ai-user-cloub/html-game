Meet Pivot.

One button. A little timing.
A browser experiment by Kenneth YWN / Wing.

Keep the ball in motion with a paddle that changes direction every time you press.

  • Tap or press Space. Tap the table, use the Spin paddle button, or press Space while the table is focused.
  • Find the rhythm. Hit a ball with the moving paddle to score. If any ball falls out, the round ends.
  • Make room for more. A new ball arrives at 5, 10, 20, 40 points and beyond, up to ten balls.

Your best score stays in this browser. Switching tabs pauses the game.
