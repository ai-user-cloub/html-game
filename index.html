THE BEAUTY IS IN THE BASICS

One button.
Endless “one more.”

No complicated controls. Just a small challenge
that gets a little bigger with every hit.

  1. 01

    Tap. Spin. Repeat.

    Tap the table or press space to spin the paddle. Each press reverses its direction.

    ↻
  2. 02

    Keep the rhythm.

    Strike the ball while the paddle is moving to score. If a ball falls out, the round is over.

    ↗
  3. 03

    Make room for more.

    Unlock another ball at 5, 10, 20, 40 points and beyond. More balls. More beautiful chaos.
